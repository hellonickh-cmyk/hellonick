Side A counts the patients.
Side B prices the same patients.

Most organizations never notice they're talking about one person.

When a patient drops off, Commercial calls it an access problem.
Finance calls it a forecast miss.

Same event. Same patient. Two languages.

And neither room hears the other one.

So each team works on its own version of the problem, and the number keeps missing. It will keep missing until both rooms can describe that patient in the same words.

Why I made these changes

  • I put the hook on its own lines.So the opening survives the "…see more" cut-off instead of disappearing into a paragraph.
  • I added a concrete scene."Commercial calls it an access problem. Finance calls it a forecast miss." Readers recognise their own meetings.
  • I moved the problem ahead of the sessions.The reader feels why the number keeps missing before the offer appears.
  • I gave each bullet a short label."One event, two names" is easy to scan. The detail sits underneath.
  • I ended on a question."Which two teams describe the same miss differently?" gives people a reason to comment.
  • I took the links out of the post.They can go in the first comment, where they don't limit how many people see the post.
  • I built the image around one picture.A record split into Side A and Side B, with the patient at the centre. The image explains the post instead of repeating it.
  • I numbered the points around the record.Readers follow 1 to 7, so they spend longer on the post.
  • I left out buttons and URLs.Without them, the image reads as an insight rather than an ad.

Why consistency matters

One good post helps. A consistent look across every post helps more.

  • Use the same brand colours every time.People start to recognise the post as yours before they read the name.
  • Keep one style of image or infographic.The same layout and illustration style, post after post, becomes a signature people remember.
  • Recognition builds trust.When followers know what a post from you looks like, they stop scrolling for it and expect it to be worth their time.
